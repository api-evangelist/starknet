This article explores practical applications of STRK20, Starknet's privacy-enabling token standard. It presents eleven buildable products from private payments and payroll to sealed auctions and agent wallets, demonstrating how privacy integrated into existing workflows can enhance security and user control without requiring separate platforms or specialized knowledge.
